Abstract

We present a pre-training approach for vision and lan-
guage transformer models, which is based on a mixture of
diverse tasks. We explore both the use of image-text cap-
tioning data in pre-training, which does not need additional
supervision, as well as object-aware strategies to pre-train
the model. We evaluate the method on a number of text-
generative vision+language tasks, such as Visual Question
Answering, visual entailment and captioning, and demon-
strate large gains over standard pre-training methods.
